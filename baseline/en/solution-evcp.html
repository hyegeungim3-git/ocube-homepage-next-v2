EVCP

Chargers, usage, payment and energy data joined into one — with AI predicting faults and demand, and tuning how you operate.

OverviewKey featuresService structureReferenceWhere it fits
Overview

Charging data, turned into steadier and leaner operation

EVCP brings together charger integration, membership, payment and monitoring, and adds AI analysis for fault response and energy management.

Managing every chargerChargers from different makers connected over OCPP, with status and settings managed remotely
Predicting faultsCharging state and error history are read to find likely failures early, and to say what to inspect first
Membership, payment, settlementMembers, bookings, payment methods, usage records and settlement — all handled in one place
Demand and energyUsage patterns by time and place, and energy consumption, inform planning, pricing and load management
AI Operations Console

Operations and AI analysis on one screen

Site and charger status is read together with usage data, giving what you need to respond to faults and run the network better.

Monitoring

Site monitoring

See every site and charger on the map, and open the detail and usage history straight away.

  • By operator and region Pick the site from a list by operator or region
  • Status and history per charger Charger status and recent usage in one view
  • Straight from the map Choose the site on the map itself
Dashboard

Operations dashboard

Arrange the charts you want, and watch charger status, revenue and energy delivered as it happens.

  • Monthly revenue Revenue trend by period
  • Energy delivered Monthly totals show the scale of the operation
  • Chargers in service By region — available, charging, offline and faulty
AI Insight

Predicting faults, demand and energy

Charging state, error history, usage patterns and energy consumption are read by AI to suggest what the operator should look at first.

  • Early warning Repeated errors and shifting status put the most likely failures at the top of the list
  • Demand forecast Patterns by hour, day and area show expected demand and where it will be busy
  • Energy recommendations Consumption and tariffs taken together to spread load and adjust operating hours
Event

Live event monitoring

Charger events are collected live, shown in a list and on the map, and timed to the moment the state changed.

  • Events as they happen Start, idle, disconnect and the rest, recorded in the order they occurred
  • Where it happened The site is shown on the map at the same time
  • Change history Charger ID, site name and the time of the change
Search

Finding a site on the map

Roaming partner sites and their chargers can be searched on the map. The driver app and the admin app share the same map module.

  • Site and charger detail Detail for each site and charger, from the map
  • Search by address Find a site by typing the address
  • Filter by operator and type Filter by operator and by charger type — slow, fast and ultra-fast
Service Architecture

Separate services, so a fault stays small and growth stays easy

Membership, charger integration (OCPP), payment and roaming each stand alone — limiting the blast radius of a fault and making it simple to add what you need.
