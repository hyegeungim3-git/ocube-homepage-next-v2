Overview

Turning what the vehicles record into better operation

Driving and diagnostic data show risky driving — and how you are doing on cost and carbon.

Driving and safety analysisSpeed, harsh acceleration and hard braking are read to find risky driving and where it can improve
Energy and chargingBattery and energy use are watched, and charging and discharging schedules tuned
Carbon managementDistance and energy data give the emissions figure — and the reduction achieved

Features

AI that suggests what to do next

Operators, fleet managers and drivers work from the same data to cut risk and waste.

Operations

Fleet-wide monitoring

Positions, running state, accidents, faults and risky-driving events in one view — so you see how the fleet is moving and what needs attention first.

  • Live overview Position, utilisation, availability and incidents, on a map and in figures
  • Risky stretches Where harsh acceleration, braking and cornering keep happening — with the weather and road conditions alongside
  • Comparing performance Safety, fuel and efficiency compared on the same basis, by vehicle, driver and route
Fleet

Vehicle and driver level

Each vehicle and driver accounted for, with maintenance, dispatch and deployment adjusted around fault signs and demand.

  • Vehicle and driver status Driving score, fuel, utilisation, distance and event history, per unit
  • Preventive maintenance Diagnostic and CAN signals show what may fail and when to service it
  • Planning and records Deployment adjusted to demand, with trip records and what was done kept together
Management

Results and the case for investing

Fuel savings, avoided maintenance and driver incentives add up to a monthly figure — and to the numbers the next investment decision needs.

  • The monthly picture Fuel savings, avoided maintenance and incentives, netted off for the month
  • Savings over time How savings have moved since it went in, period by period
  • Distribution and benchmark Performance by driver and vehicle group, set against comparable operations
Driver

Coaching for each driver

Driving behaviour explained in the light of road, weather and operating conditions — with what to change for safety and efficiency.

  • Judged in context Congestion, weather and conditions taken into account, giving something fairer than a bare score
  • Coaching you can act on Idling, holding a steady speed, easing off before the lights — things a driver can do today
  • Did it work Safety score, fuel and energy use compared before and after the coaching
Carbon

Carbon and energy savings

Emissions savings are worked out from recorded distance and fuel economy — and how driving habits affect that economy is looked at too.

  • Based on distance and economy Savings calculated from the tachograph’s distance and fuel economy
  • Safe driving and fuel economy How habits move fuel economy, expressed as a correlation
  • Over time Improvement accumulated period by period
Verification

Proving the effect

Compared against what would have happened without AI, and across driver groups before and after coaching — so the improvement has evidence behind it.

  • Against doing nothing The same conditions, without AI, as the point of comparison
  • Group by group Drivers split into groups and compared before and after coaching
  • Cross-checked On-board diagnostics checked against the tachograph, so the numbers hold
